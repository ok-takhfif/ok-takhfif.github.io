\# ViraNode Landing Page — Context



A running glossary of resolved terms and decisions for the redesign. Not a spec — just shared vocabulary so we don't drift.



\## Glossary



\### Site Purpose

Lead generation. The site's job is to convert visitors into project inquiries / client relationships — not to serve as a vanity portfolio or internal-only page.



\### Target Audience

Staged rollout:

\- \*\*Stage 1 (now):\*\* Iran-based founders and investors who have an idea for a platform or app and need a technical build partner to execute it. ViraNode is the team that turns their idea into a real product.

\- \*\*Stage 2 (future):\*\* International clients outsourcing dev/engineering work.

The current redesign should serve Stage 1. Stage 2 needs are noted but not designed for yet.



\### Primary Language

Farsi (Persian), RTL layout, written for the actual Stage 1 audience rather than translated from English marketing copy. English is a secondary toggle held in reserve for Stage 2 — not built now.



\### Content Structure

Restructured around the founder's journey rather than an "about us" shape:

Hero (the pitch) → Problem/Process (how ViraNode turns an idea into a real product) → Proof/Work (past builds, even placeholder for now) → Team → Start a Project (contact/CTA).

Team and contact still appear, but they support the pitch rather than being the pitch themselves.



\### Visual Direction

Team name stays ViraNode — not up for change. The "node" concept (network of connected points, matching the name) stays as the core visual idea, but its expression moves away from generic sci-fi/particle-network tropes toward Persian geometric pattern logic (structural influence, not literal ornamentation) — this is where genuine differentiation for the Stage 1 Iranian audience lives, versus reskinning a trope every other dev agency already uses.

Literalness: minimal/abstracted. Girih-style tessellation (interlocking geometric line-work, star-and-polygon angles) used as a modern graphic system — node connections resolve into girih-derived angles instead of random particle scatter. No literal ornamental motifs, borders, or tourism-style decoration. Existing blue/amber palette lineage (echoing traditional turquoise-and-gold) carries forward rather than being reinvented.



\### Typography

Mixed register: headlines use a warmer/more traditional-flavored Farsi typeface, body text uses a modern/geometric Farsi typeface (e.g. Vazirmatn-style). Headlines carry the cultural warmth, body text carries the technical-credibility signal.



\### Proof/Work Section

Real projects, not placeholders. First confirmed project:

\- \*\*Ok Tafrih B2B\*\* — a supplier-facing B2B panel for the Ok Tafrih travel/leisure platform (login portal for suppliers to manage travel deals/bookings). Currently under development. Link: https://b2b.oktafrih.com, shown as a quiet secondary link on the card (not the main CTA).

A second project may be added later; only Ok Tafrih B2B is confirmed for now.

General rule for this section going forward: project links are always optional/secondary, never the primary CTA — not all future clients will want their project publicly linked, so the card design shouldn't depend on a link being present.



\### Team Size

4 people, 4 distinct roles (matches the original placeholder card count). Real names/roles/bios/photos to be supplied later — mock/placeholder cards continue to be used for now.
